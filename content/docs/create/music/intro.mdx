## Vision

The goal of this section is to help you use your Launchpad not just as a performance device, but as an instrument and control surface inside a full music production setup.

Planned topics include:

- Using Launchpad for clip launching and arrangement in Ableton Live
- Finger drumming as a way to sketch drum ideas
- Mapping macros, FX, and mixer controls to Launchpad
- Working with virtual instruments and VSTs
- Recording your performances as MIDI/audio and turning them into full tracks

## For Now

While this section is being built out, you can:

- Reuse techniques from the **Create (Lightshows)** section for sample selection and project organization
- Explore general music production tutorials focused on Ableton Live, FL Studio, or other DAWs
- Join the community Discord to share ideas and ask for workflow feedback
