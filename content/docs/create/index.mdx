Find resources and guides to help you create your own lightshows, including tutorials, templates, and best practices from experienced creators in the community.

The first step when considering the creation of your own performances/covers is whether you want to make a project with samples and lights, or place your focus on just the lightshow.

## Decision: Lightshow Only vs Samples + Lightshow

Depending on your goals, you can choose whether you want to have a performance with samples or only a lightshow. Here are the key differences:

### Lightshow Only

- One main audio track (the full song or a stem)
- MIDI tracks mainly drive lights and FX
- Focus is on visual choreography and tight timing

### Samples + Lightshow

- Multiple audio tracks with one-shots, chops, and FX
- More MIDI tracks for triggering both sound and lights
- Layouts designed for live variation and improvisation

If you choose **Samples + Lightshow**, go to the [**Getting Started**](create/performances/) page of the performances section. It will guide you through the entire creation process.

If you choose **Lightshow Only**, proceed to the [**Lights**](create/performances/lights/) section to learn how to build your lightshow project from start to finish.
