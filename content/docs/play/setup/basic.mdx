title: Basic Setup

Before diving into complex projects, make sure your setup is stable.

## Hardware

- Connect your Launchpad directly to your computer if possible.
- If you need a hub, use a **powered USB hub** to avoid power issues.
- Use a good-quality USB cable and avoid moving the device during play.

## Drivers & Firmware

- Install any required drivers for your Launchpad model from the official Novation website.
- Update the firmware to the latest version using Novation’s tools.

## DAW / App Configuration

For **Ableton Live**:

- Enable your Launchpad in Preferences → Link/MIDI.
- Set the appropriate **Control Surface**, **Input**, and **Output** for your model.
- Make sure Track and Remote are enabled on the relevant MIDI ports.

For **UniPad / Unitor / others**:

- Follow the app’s specific setup instructions.
- Confirm that the correct Launchpad model and mode are selected.

## Latency & Performance

- Lower your audio buffer size until you notice crackles, then back off slightly.
- Close unnecessary background apps.
- If using many visual plugins or effects, test stability before recording or streaming.

## Quality-of-Life Tips

- Save a **default project template** with your Launchpad already configured.
- Keep backups of your project files in cloud storage or version control.
- Label your projects clearly by difficulty and Launchpad model.
