---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Common issues and solutions for setting up Launchpad projects.
---

- If your Launchpad X or Pro MK3 is set up correctly but has weird flickering and doesn't play correctly, it might be a common bug related to Control Surfaces in Ableton. Go to Preferences and set the Control Surfaces of your Launchpad to "None".

- USB Connection issues: Novation Driver not installed or faulty USB port/cable. Make sure you plug in the Launchpad directly and not through a USB hub.
