title: Recommended Models
description: Suggested Launchpad models for different skill levels and use cases.
---

We recommend three Launchpad/MIDI Controller models: the Launchpad Pro MK2 (often referred to as "OG Pro"), the Launchpad X and the Mystrix by 203 Electronics.

## Launchpad Pro MK2 (OG Pro)
Pros:
- Allows for custom firmware (CFW)
- Easy to customize (spraypaint, skins, etc.)
- Durable and repairable (replacement parts can be bought directly from Novation)

Cons:
- Very chunky and heavy, less portable
- USB port is prone to breaking, leading to connectivity issues
- Discontinued. Finding one in adequate condition may be difficult

## Launchpad X
Pros:
- More portable and lightweight
- Larger and softer pads, usually feels nicer to play on

Cons:
- No custom firmware support (yet)
- A bit harder to disassemble for repairs or modifications

## Mystrix by 203 Electronics
Pros:
- Compact and portable design
- Novel features such as touchbar or underlights
- Fully open-source firmware for customization
- Great for starters

Cons:
- Pads might not feel as good as Novation's Launchpads
- Limited documentation and community support compared to Novation models
- Fewer apps specifically designed for it

# Alternative Models

## Launchpad Mini MK3
Pros:
- Very portable, lightweight, and small
- Great for starters who would like to test the waters without spending too much

Cons:
- Smaller size may be uncomfortable to play on
- Upgrading to a better model (Pro MK2, X, Mystrix) for dual launchpad performances may not fare well due to size differences
- Pads depress, meaning more force is required to activate the pads

# Models to Avoid

The Launchpad models mentioned below are plagued with issues that make them worse compared to recommended/alternative models mentioned above.

## Launchpad Pro MK3
Pros:
- Great for Music Production
- Slim and portable
- Larger and softer pads, usually feels nicer to play on

Cons:
- No custom firmware support (yet)
- **FPS is locked**, resulting in choppy and slow lightshows
- Extremely sensitive pads that may affect performances
- A bit harder to disassembe for repairs or modifications

Recommended alternative: Launchpad Pro MK2 (OG Pro)

## Launchpad MK2
Pros:
- Easy to customize (spraypaint, skins, etc.)
- Durable and repairable (replacement parts can be bought directly from Novation)

Cons:
- Poor and sensitive USB port that causes the Launchpad to frequently disconnect even with the slightest movement
- Pads depress, meaning more force is required to activate the pads
- Discontinued. Finding one in adequate condition may be difficult

Recommended alternative: Launchpad X

## Launchpad S/Launchpad Classic
- Discontinued. Finding one in adequate condition may be difficult
- Limited colours (Red, Green, and Yellow only)

Recommended alternative: Launchpad X

## Launchpad Mini MK1/Launchpad Mini MK2
- Discontinued. Finding one in adequate condition may be difficult
- Limited colours (Red, Green, and Yellow only)
- Pads depress, meaning more force is required to activate the pads
