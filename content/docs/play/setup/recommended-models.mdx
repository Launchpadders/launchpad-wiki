title: Recommended Models
description: Suggested Launchpad models for different skill levels and use cases.
---

We recommend three Launchpad/MIDI Controller models: the Launchpad Pro MK2 (often referred to as "OG Pro"), the Launchpad X and the Mystrix by 203 Electronics.

## Launchpad Pro MK2 (OG Pro)
Pros:
- Allows for custom firmware (CFW)
- Easy to customize (spraypaint, skins, etc.)
- Durable and repairable (replacement parts can be bought directly from Novation)

Cons:
- Very chunky and heavy, less portable
- USB port is prone to breaking, leading to connectivity issues

## Launchpad X
Pros:
- More portable and lightweight
- Larger and softer pads, usually feels nicer to play on

Cons:
- No custom firmware support (yet)
- A bit harder to disassemble for repairs or modifications

## Mystrix by 203 Electronics
Pros:
- Compact and portable design
- Novel features such as touchbar or underlights
- Fully open-source firmware for customization

Cons:
- Pads might not feel as good as Novation's Launchpads
- Limited documentation and community support compared to Novation models
- Fewer apps specifically designed for it
