Welcome to the Launchpad community, provided by the Launchpad community.

We aim to provide you with the most comprehensive resource about the Novation Launchpad MIDI controller. From playing lightshow performances to creating your own or even making music, we want to cover all of it and help you get started or improve your skills.

## Start by Playing Existing Projects

If you are interested in the lightshow performance part, we strongly recommend starting by playing project files created by others. This helps you:

- Get a feel for common layouts and techniques
- Learn how songs are structured across the pads
- Build muscle memory before designing your own setups

## Skillsets for Performance


### Rhythm & Timing

- Practice with a metronome or the track’s click
- Start slow, then gradually increase BPM
- Record yourself and listen back for timing issues

### Visual Coordination

Lightshows are as visual as they are musical:

- Learn to anticipate where pads will light up
- Practice “looking ahead” instead of chasing lights



## Setup Guides, Tips & Tricks

Before you dive too deep, make sure your setup is stable. Key topics:
