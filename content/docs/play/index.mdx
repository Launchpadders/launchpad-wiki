Welcome to the Launchpad Wiki, provided by The Launchpad Community.

Here we aim to provide you with the most comprehensive resource about the Novation Launchpad MIDI controller. From playing lightshow performances to creating your own or even making music, we want to cover all of it and help you get started or improve your skills.

Join the community on Discord to ask questions, share projects, and get feedback:

- [Launchpad Community Discord](https://discord.gg/XzUm2ZAk33)
