These beginner-friendly projects help you build confidence and understand how to play Launchpad performances.
Beginner projects usually feature very simple drum patterns, simple sample placement and slow tempo. 

Another useful thing to look out for is the inclusion of a tutorial video or MIDI tutorial included with the project, which allows you to learn the project more easily.
