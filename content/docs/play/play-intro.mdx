## Why Start by Playing Existing Projects?

Playing projects made by others is the fastest way to:

- Understand how Launchpad layouts are commonly structured
- Get used to the feel of the pads and timing
- Experience advanced lightshows before you can build them yourself

## What You Need

- A **Novation Launchpad** (any modern model works: Mini, X, Pro, MK2, etc.)
- A computer with **Ableton Live** or a compatible app (UniPad, Unitor, etc.)
- Project files or performance videos to follow along with

## Recommended First Steps

1. **Pick a beginner-friendly project** (see the Beginner Projects page).
2. **Follow the creator’s instructions** for loading the project into Ableton or the target app.
3. **Slow the track down** if needed so you can focus on accuracy.
4. **Loop small sections** and repeat until they feel comfortable.

From here, explore the dedicated pages on beginner projects, performance skills, and setup.
