Welcome to the **Finding Projects** section! Here, you can explore a variety of Launchpad projects created by the vibrant community. Whether you're looking for beginner-friendly lightshows or expert-level performances, you'll find something to inspire your own creations.

## Where to Find Projects

Many creators share their Launchpad performances on YouTube. Search for keywords like "Launchpad Cover" or "Launchpad Performance" to discover new projects.

You can also join the Launchpad Community Discord server, where members frequently share their projects and collaborate.

Moreover, Denking has a project archive, which contains a collection of Launchpad projects which are not available on YouTube anymore: [Denking's Project Archive](https://lpd.duuuuuuuden.ru/).
