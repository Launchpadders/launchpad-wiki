Most creators share their Launchpad performances on YouTube (or for Chinese Launchpadders, on Bilibili). Search for keywords like "Launchpad Cover" or "Launchpad Performance" to discover new projects.

You can also join the Launchpad Community Discord server, where members frequently share their projects and collaborate.

Moreover, Denking has a project archive, which contains a collection of Launchpad projects which are not available on YouTube anymore: [Denking's Project Archive](https://lpd.duuuuuuuden.ru/).
